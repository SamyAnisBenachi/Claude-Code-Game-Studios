The user is the creative director and game designer for Lanes and Lies. They have strong design instincts and a clear reference aesthetic (Ankama/Wakfu). They ask for opinionated, specific direction — not vague principles. When asking for AD output for implementation-gating sections (e.g., GDD Visual/Audio Requirements), they want spec-level detail: exact durations, frame counts, color references, priority tiers, and specific constraints the implementer cannot misinterpret.

They work iteratively: question-first, options-second, decision-third. However, when they have already defined the scope of what they need (as in the Combat Resolution VA section request), they expect the AD to deliver concrete creative direction directly rather than asking more clarifying questions about style — the art bible already answers those.
