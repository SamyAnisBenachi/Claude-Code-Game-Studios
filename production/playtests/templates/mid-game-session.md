## Success/Fail Criteria

### Success Criteria

- [ ] Tester can explain current match pressure and next decision.
- [ ] Auction and economy create at least one meaningful read/drop/bid moment.
- [ ] Deck, class, or lane plan shows signs of coming online.
- [ ] Non-input phases provide useful information to read.
- [ ] No observed mid-game system creates a pillar-critical idle or agency loss.

### Fail Criteria

- [ ] Tester cannot understand current state well enough to choose a plan.
- [ ] Auction feels like a side menu rather than the signature tension point.
- [ ] Economy advantage removes meaningful agency for one player.
- [ ] Deck/class progression feels invisible, random, or trapping.
- [ ] A no-input phase feels like dead time with no meaningful information.
- [ ] A blocking bug prevents enough evidence collection.
