## Success/Fail Criteria

### Success Criteria

- [ ] Tester can state the basic goal in their own words.
- [ ] Tester makes at least one meaningful decision without strategy coaching.
- [ ] Tester understands at least one source of opponent-read information.
- [ ] Tester reaches and understands at least one auction or the report explains
      why this was blocked.
- [ ] No repeated confusion loop prevents normal play.

### Fail Criteria

- [ ] Tester cannot identify what they are trying to accomplish after the first
      play segment.
- [ ] Tester cannot make a decision without direct instruction.
- [ ] Core UI or rules produce a repeated confusion loop.
- [ ] The first auction is unreadable or feels irrelevant.
- [ ] A blocking bug prevents enough evidence collection.
