## Success/Fail Criteria

### Success Criteria

- [ ] Pressure escalates from early to mid/late game in a readable way.
- [ ] The tester can explain what caused major swings.
- [ ] Losing or trailing state still leaves meaningful decisions for at least one
      recovery window.
- [ ] Auction agency remains meaningful under resource pressure.
- [ ] Match length and pacing are close to the intended 10-15 minute game target
      or deviations are explained by recorded evidence.

### Fail Criteria

- [ ] Difficulty is flat, with no meaningful escalation.
- [ ] Difficulty spikes are sudden and not readable from game state.
- [ ] The leading player snowballs without meaningful counterplay.
- [ ] The trailing player loses auction agency for extended periods.
- [ ] Hidden objectives stop mattering before the endgame.
- [ ] A blocking bug prevents enough evidence collection.
